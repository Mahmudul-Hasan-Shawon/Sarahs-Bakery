Our Story

Baking Has Always Been
Sarah's Love Language

What started as birthday cakes for family in a small Dhaka kitchen has grown into a bakery trusted by hundreds of happy customers — without ever losing the handmade touch that started it all.

Since 2019

From a Home Kitchen to Dhaka's Doorsteps

Sarah's Bakery began the way most good bakeries do — with a home oven, a few trusted recipes, and orders from friends who kept asking for "just one more cake." Today we bake every order fresh, in small batches, using real butter, real cream, and no shortcuts.

We still test every new flavor in-house before it goes on the menu, and every custom cake is sketched, priced, and confirmed with you personally over WhatsApp before it's baked — because a cake for your celebration deserves that kind of care.

Every order is baked to order, packed with insulated boxes, and handed off with tracking so you always know exactly when your cake will arrive.

What We Believe In

Four simple principles guide every cake that leaves our kitchen.

Real Ingredients

Real butter, real cream, and fresh eggs — never shortening or artificial whipped topping.

Baked to Order

Nothing sits in a freezer waiting for a buyer. Your cake is baked after you order it, not before.

Personal Custom Work

Custom designs are discussed directly with Sarah over WhatsApp, from sketch to final flavor.

Careful Delivery

Every cake travels in a padded, insulated box with a tracking code you can follow in real time.
